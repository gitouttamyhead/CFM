Telegram Reminders

Optional study reminders between Sundays — when new material is ready and gentle nudges during the week. One-way only; no need to reply.

Step 1 — Get Telegram

Telegram is a free messaging app (separate from this website). Install it on your phone or computer:

iPhone / iPad Android Desktop

Step 2 — Open our bot

Search for this bot in Telegram, or tap the link below on the device where Telegram is installed:

@Come_follow_me_helper_bot
Open Come Follow Me Helper

The bot display name is Come Follow Me Helper. Make sure the username matches exactly.

Step 3 — Subscribe

  1. Open the bot chat and tap Start (or send /start).
  2. You’ll get a short welcome message — you’re enrolled.
  3. Reminders will appear in Telegram when new study material is posted.

To stop reminders anytime, send /stop to the bot. Send /start again to rejoin.
